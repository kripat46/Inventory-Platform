Title: Operational Guidelines for the Enterprise Supply Chain Control Tower Platform
1. System Access & Interface Verification
Step 1.1: Open the verified corporate Streamlit Cloud web application URL.
2. Submitting Queries & Unstructured Data Processing
Step 2.1: Locate the AI Strategic Advisor chat box in the left sidebar console.
Step 2.2: Input your standard prompt or copy-paste any unstructured text file assets (such as incident reports, email complaints, or market calendar disruptions).
Stage an optimized replenishment for DEPT-3.
Analyze our latest electronics supplier disruption logs and see how it impacts DEPT-3 stockout liabilities.
Review our historical incident report batch logs for any unrecorded supply chain anomalies matching our current deficits.
Read our market calendar data and competitor price war files to assess our retail shelf risk profiles.
3. Human-in-the-Loop Fulfillment Authorization
Step 3.1: Review the allocation proposals staged under the top Replenishment Canopy panel block.
Step 3.2: Evaluate network constraints based on current safety reserves:Option A (Multi-Hub Network Split): Click to authorize if the system approves warehouse stock margins. Option B (Emergency Manufacturing Plant Run): Click to execute an emergency production line run if warehouse margins fall below the mandatory 5,000-unit floor cushion.
4. Storefront Dock Ingestion Control
Step 4.1: Upon the arrival of logistical transport vehicles at the physical storefront bay, locate the respective vehicle row inside your bottom Real-Time Simulation Shipment Ledger.
Step 4.2: Click Log Delivery Arrival button for that shipment.
Step 4.3: System Impact Pass: The engine locks the button to prevent duplicate entries, writes a new auditable transaction row to the datastore, runs a greedy search routine to clear active stockout rows, scales your green bar charts, drops penalty card metrics.
